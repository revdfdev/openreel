OpenReel is a lightweight, open-source media player.

Features:
* Play local video and audio files
* Play network streams and shares (HTTP/HLS, SMB, FTP, DLNA)
* Download torrents and stream them while they download
* Send files to nearby devices over local Wi-Fi (no internet or account needed)
* Hardware and software decoding, external and styled subtitles, audio sync,
  playback speed, gesture controls, background audio

OpenReel has no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no cloud services. The
torrent feature uses the BitTorrent protocol; please only download content you
are legally entitled to. Local sharing works directly between devices on the
same network and sends nothing to any server.

Powered by mpv (libmpv) for playback and libtorrent for downloads.
